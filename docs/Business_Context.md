# Business Context

## Overview

ForecastIQ is an Enterprise Forecast Intelligence Platform designed to help organizations accurately forecast future demand using statistical forecasting, machine learning, and foundation models.

The platform enables business users, analysts, and decision-makers to transform historical operational data into actionable business insights for planning, optimization, and strategic decision-making.

---

# Business Problem

Many organizations struggle to answer questions such as:

- How much demand should we expect next week or next month?
- How many support cases will be created?
- How many agents are required?
- What happens if sales increase by 20%?
- What is the impact of promotions and holidays?
- Which forecasting model performs best?

Traditional spreadsheet-based forecasting often fails to capture seasonality, business events, and external drivers, leading to inaccurate forecasts and poor planning decisions.

---

# Business Objectives

ForecastIQ addresses these challenges by providing:

- Accurate demand forecasting
- Explainable AI-based forecasting
- Statistical and Machine Learning model comparison
- Scenario planning using What-if Simulation
- Business-friendly dashboards
- Forecast confidence intervals
- Downloadable reports
