# Business Recommendations

## Purpose

Forecasting is valuable only when it enables better business decisions.

ForecastIQ transforms forecasting outputs into actionable recommendations that help organizations improve operational planning, reduce costs, and increase customer satisfaction.

# Operational Recommendations

## Workforce Planning

Business Insight

Forecasted demand increases by 18%.

Recommendation

Increase staffing by 15–20% during peak weeks.

Expected Benefit

- Reduced customer waiting time
- Improved service level
- Lower overtime costs

---

## Promotion Planning

Business Insight

Promotional campaigns increase case volume significantly.

Recommendation

Allocate additional support agents before campaign launch.

Expected Benefit

- Improved customer experience
- Reduced backlog

---

## Contact Ratio Monitoring

Business Insight

Increasing Contact Ratio directly increases operational demand.

Recommendation

Continuously monitor Contact Ratio and update workforce plans accordingly.

Expected Benefit

Improved forecast accuracy.

---

## Holiday Planning

Business Insight

Public holidays create demand fluctuations.

Recommendation

Include holiday calendars in forecasting models.

Expected Benefit

More stable staffing plans.

---

## Forecast Accuracy Monitoring

Business Insight

Forecast accuracy may decrease due to changing business conditions.

Recommendation

Monitor forecasting KPIs weekly and retrain models when required.

Expected Benefit

Consistently accurate forecasts.
